About

Frontend Simplified is a free, open-source platform that teaches you how to build beautiful websites from scratch. We believe that learning frontend development should be simple and accessible to everyone.

The platform is designed to be beginner-friendly, with a focus on practical, hands-on learning. You'll learn by building real-world projects, and you'll have access to a supportive community of learners and mentors who can help you along the way.

Social

Welcome to the Social section of Frontend Simplified! Here, you'll find a vibrant community of learners and mentors who are passionate about frontend development. Whether you're just starting out or looking to level up your skills, our community is here to support you every step of the way.

Need a mentor? Our experienced mentors are available to provide guidance, answer your questions, and help you navigate the world of frontend development. You can connect with mentors through our community forums, live chat, or scheduled mentorship sessions.
